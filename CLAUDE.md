# baixue-wu.github.io

Baixue Wu's personal website (GitHub Pages, repo `Baixue-Wu/baixue-wu.github.io`). It targets recruiters for AI product / AI content operations roles in China.

Positioning 以算法与数据测量内容，以视听与审美理解用户 lives in form only: light, colour and motion (data in cyan, film in amber, the scanner in the hero). Content is grouped by type in plain language a recruiter understands: 项目, 视频作品, 论文与课题, then honours, tools, contact.

## Structure

- `index.html`, `css/site.css`: the page. Unconfirmed text is a `<mark class="todo">【待补…】</mark>`.
- `js/hero.js`: the hero reel on canvas. Frames run past a scanner that follows the pointer; left of it each frame is drawn as its k-means palette, right of it as the image.
- `js/site.js`: film barcodes as video scrubbers, durations, slogan underlines. Both scripts read `media/media.json`.
- `media/`: generated by `site-extract` from `media/sources.json` (frames, palettes, barcodes, transcoded videos and posters). Committed so the site needs no build.
- `assets/cards/`: card visuals generated by `site-render`. `assets/fonts/`: WOFF2 subsets generated by `site-fonts`; rerun it after changing page text or new characters render in a fallback font.
- `tools/site_assets/`: the three generators, each with `--help`. `fonts/`: source fonts, not committed.

## Rules

- No external hosts at runtime: Google Fonts and most CDNs are blocked or slow in mainland China. Fonts are self-hosted subsets.
- Show only Baixue's own works and public-domain Sherlock Jr.; no Marvel-footage commentary videos.
- Visuals that illustrate a method without her data are labelled 示意动画 and carry no result numbers.
- Her contribution is stated only as far as confirmed; the rest stays 【待补】.
- Author and commit as Baixue Wu <baixuewu0@gmail.com> via the `github-baixue` SSH alias. No assistant attribution. Comments and docstrings avoid em dashes.

Design decisions: `design-decisions.md`.

## Public project demos

`InkMuse/`, `CineAtlas/`, `NextHook/` are generated static builds copied with `tools/site-demos.py --help`. Rebuild in their source repositories; do not hand-edit compiled files. Manifests identify the source revision and artifact hashes. InkMuse exposes samples, editing, local uploads and export; NextHook exposes review and manual exploration with query-string navigation; AI service operations are omitted from these public builds. `.nojekyll` preserves generated assets on GitHub Pages.
